Knowledge accrues like interest, over time.

Maddox MADDMIX Chhim is a multi-platinum mix engineer. He has mixed projects for artists like 21 Savage, Tyga, Famous Dex, Kid Ink, Labrinth, and many more.

Originally from Boiling Springs South Carolina, Maddox started working in the music industry as a mixing assistant for Jaycen Joshua. After 3 1/2 years of assisting Maddox then went to work on his own as a mixing engineer working landing major projects.

Maddox continues to work building relationships within the industry and is currently working in Atlanta, GA. He is more than qualified for the job! His mixes are industry ready and will compete with anything in the major market.

“Atlanta is the urban music center of the world, and as the new kid on the block of a sizeable music scene, Maddox could easily have struggled after arriving. Luckily, his previous experience has given him a head start.”
Paul Tingen

Read more about Maddox on "Sound on Sound"

Maddox has mixed music across the country from San Francisco, CA all the way to Atlanta, GA. He has worked with top talent as well as rising stars in the music industry, placing him directly at the heart of the music scene to date.

After a great beginning to his carreer encompassing almost a decade of working with top artist, Maddox has setup shop in Atlanta, GA to continue is his art and work as a top Mix Engineer in the area.

Maddox has also partnered with to start teaching his skills to new mix artists with School of Sonics.
